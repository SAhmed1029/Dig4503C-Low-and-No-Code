A passion for perfect crepes since 2015

Made with Love & Tradition

Welcome to La Petite Crêperie! We started with a simple dream: to bring the authentic taste of French crêpes to our community. Every crepe we make is crafted with care, using recipes passed down through generations.

Our batter is made fresh daily with the finest ingredients – farm-fresh eggs, premium flour, and real butter. Whether you're craving something sweet or savory, we've got the perfect crepe waiting for you!
